A few clear answers before the first conversation.

If your question is not here, send us a note. The right first step is usually a direct one.

Questions
01

What stage do you invest in?

We focus on companies with a clear product or service, real customer insight and a credible path to durable growth. We are more interested in the quality of the opportunity than a single stage label.

02

What sectors interest you?

Our focus includes technology and software, financial services, healthcare and life sciences, and business services. We follow founders solving meaningful problems in changing markets.

03

How should a founder get in touch?

Email hello@aurevia.ventures with a concise overview of the company, the customer problem and what you are building next. A deck is welcome but not required for a first note.

04

What does your support look like?

It depends on the company. We can help with strategic decisions, senior hiring, positioning, commercial introductions and the operating questions that appear as a business grows.
